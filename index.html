Frequently Asked Questions

How does the scoring work?

Our AI analyzes your CV based on key industry metrics including ATS readability, keywords, impact verbs, and clarity. The score reflects how well your CV aligns with job market standards.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Your CV is processed securely and transiently for analysis. We do not store your CV or personal details on our servers; all analysis results remain locally on your device.

Can I restore my purchase?

Yes, you can restore your purchases at any time from the Settings menu in the app using the "Restore Purchases" button.
