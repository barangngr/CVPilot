Privacy Policy

Last updated: Jan 17, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to CVPilot. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy informs you about how we handle your data when you use our application.

2. Data We Collect

When you use CVPilot, we may collect the following types of information:

  • CV Data: The text and content of the CVs you upload for analysis.
  • Usage Data: Anonymous data about how you use the app to help us improve the service.
  • Purchase History: Information related to your separate in-app purchases (processed securely by Apple).

3. Data Storage and Security

Local Storage: Your analysis results and history are stored locally on your device. We do not maintain a cloud database of your personal analysis history.

Transient Processing: When you submit a CV for analysis, it is transmitted securely to our analysis engine solely for the purpose of generating the report. The content of your CV is not retained on our servers after the analysis is complete.

4. How We Use Your Data

We use your transient data solely for:

  • Providing the real-time CV analysis service.
  • Improving our AI algorithms (anonymized data only).
  • Customer support.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We may use trusted third-party services (like our cloud infrastructure providers) solely for the purpose of operating the app.
